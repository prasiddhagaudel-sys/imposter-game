# 😈 Imposter v1.0.1 - Release Notes

> **Version 1.0.1 - The Mastermind Update!**

We are thrilled to release **Imposter v1.0.1**, featuring abstract high-difficulty hints, auto-rotating multilingual slogans, and a suspense thriller reveal screen!

---

## 🔥 Key Updates & Enhancements in v1.0.1

### 🧠 High-Difficulty Abstract Hints
- Replaced all easy, literal word hints across 10 categories with **abstract, subtle, and strategic clues**.
- Makes it challenging and thrilling for the Imposter to guess the secret word right away!

### 🔄 Auto-Rotating Multilingual Homepage Slogans
- Homepage slogans now rotate automatically every **4 seconds** (and on tap) with smooth fade transitions.
- Includes playful quotes in **Nepali**, **Hindi**, and **English**:
  - 🇳🇵 *"को छ त असली इम्पोस्टर? 🕵️‍♂️🔥"*
  - 🇮🇳 *"कौन है तुम्हारे बीच का गद्दार? 🕵️‍♂️🔥"*
  - 🇬🇧 *"Blend in. Deceive. Survive. 😈🔥"*

### 🎭 Thriller Imposter Reveal Screen
- **Camera Shake & Emergency Strobe**: Heart-pounding visual reveal with pulsing red light and subtle camera vibration.
- **Multilingual Villain Taglines**: Randomly displays dramatic lines in Nepali, Hindi, or English on revealed Imposter cards (*"थाहै नपाई झुक्याइदियो! 😈"*, *"पकड़े गए या सबको उल्लू बनाया? 😈"*, *"The Mastermind of Deception! 🎭"*).

### ⚙️ Settings Modal & Dropdown Guide
- Pinned Settings gear containing:
  - 🎥 **Video Animation Toggle** (ON/OFF)
  - 🎵 **Audio Control Bar** & Volume Slider
  - 🔒 **100% Offline Privacy Policy**
  - 🚪 **Exit Game Button**
  - 📖 **Expandable How-To-Play Accordion**
